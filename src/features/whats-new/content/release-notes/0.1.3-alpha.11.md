# What’s new in WinSTT

## New Read Aloud voices

- Paradee 8M is a tiny, accurate English voice that downloads in seconds.
- Qwen3-TTS Base (0.6B and 1.7B) and Fun-CosyVoice3 0.5B clone a voice from a short clip of someone speaking.
- NVIDIA Magpie TTS speaks 10 languages with 5 built-in voices.
- Kitten TTS 0.8 adds micro and mini sizes.

## Upgraded voices

- Maya1 3B replaces Orpheus: describe the voice you want in words and add emotion tags inline.
- Chatterbox Multilingual V3 replaces V2 and mispronounces far fewer words.
- Kitten nano 0.8 replaces 0.2 and runs several times faster on CPU.
- Qwen3-TTS replaces Spark-TTS.

Your saved voice choices move to the new models automatically, and the old downloads are cleaned up.
