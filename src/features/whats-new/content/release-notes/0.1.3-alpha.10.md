# What’s new in WinSTT

## New speech models

- Parakeet Ultra replaces Parakeet v3: more accurate across languages and steadier on long recordings. Your existing selection moves over automatically.
- Moonshine v2 brings true streaming recognition in tiny, small, and medium sizes plus seven language variants.
- Parakeet Redux and Audio8-ASR-Infinite add a compact CPU option and a long-form Chinese/English model.

## Better speaker labels and voice detection

- Nemotron 3 diarization replaces the previous speaker pipeline and labels speakers far more accurately.
- Silero VAD v6.2 detects speech more reliably and triggers far less often on music and noise.

## More local voices and LLMs

- Audio8 TTS 0.1B is available for Read Aloud with a ready-to-use voice.
- Granite 4.2 and Superwhisper S1-mini join the suggested Ollama models.
